History

Founded with a passion for extreme outdoor exploration, our company has grown from a small local group of river enthusiasts into a premier rafting destination. We’ve spent decades discovering the safest yet most thrilling paths through untamed canyons.

Through community dedication and respect for nature's raw power, we continue to provide world-class expeditions. Our seasoned crew ensures that every journey balances intense excitement with deep appreciation for natural river environments.
