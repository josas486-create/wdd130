Nigeria

Nigeria, officially the Federal Republic of Nigeria, is a country in West Africa. It is the most populous country in Africa, consisting of 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. Known for its rich cultural diversity, vibrant economy, and diverse landscapes ranging from savannahs to tropical rain forests, Nigeria plays a major role in continental and global affairs.
